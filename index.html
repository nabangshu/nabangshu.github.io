Nabangshu Sinha

Postdoctoral Researcher · Faculty of Advanced Life Science, Hokkaido University

I build machine-learning models for high-dimensional data that change over time, and I apply them to two very different systems: electricity markets and living cells.

My PhD (Computer Science and Mathematics, University of Camerino, 2025) developed methods for forecasting whole demand and supply curves in day-ahead electricity markets, including a monotonic autoencoder published in Applied Energy. At Hokkaido I apply the same toolkit to single-cell RNA sequencing data. I grew up in Assam and studied at NIT Silchar. Every week I write about energy, weather, single-cell biology and space, with a focus on North-East India.

Research

Energy systems

Forecasting prices, demand and full market bid curves in electricity markets.

  • Demand and supply curve forecasting
  • Echo state networks and reservoir computing
  • Renewable scenario generation
  • Portfolio and emissions under uncertainty
Single-cell biology

Machine learning on single-cell RNA sequencing (scRNA-seq) data.

  • Representation learning for cell states
  • Predicting responses to perturbations
  • Cell-type deconvolution
  • Modelling how cells change over time

The common thread: both are noisy, high-dimensional systems with structure the model has to respect, such as monotone bid curves or cells that follow developmental paths. The same methods carry across: autoencoders, generative models and models of dynamics.
